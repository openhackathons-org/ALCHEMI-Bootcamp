# V4 Icon-Style Adsorption Showcase Prompts

Art direction for every panel:

- High-quality editorial scientific illustration, closer to vector/icon art
  than rendered 3D.
- Flat dark graphite background, simplified matte shapes, clean silhouettes,
  clear edge outlines, minimal gradients, no photographic texture, no depth of
  field, no lens effects.
- Use a constrained palette: graphite and cool gray surfaces, muted red oxygen,
  white hydrogen, black/charcoal carbon, blue nitrogen only in the gas panel,
  yellow sulfur only in the poisoning panel, NVIDIA-green only for active or
  adsorbed sites.
- No embedded text, labels, numbers, logos, UI frame, equations, arrows with
  words, or brand marks.
- Leave top-left negative space for later captions.
- Scientific clarity is more important than visual realism.

## OER Panel

Create a clean icon-style scientific panel for OER adsorption intermediates.
Show exactly three muted oxide slabs side by side, evenly spaced on one
baseline. Each slab is a simplified uniform lattice: matte gray metal cations
and muted red lattice oxygens, low contrast, regular surface rows. On slab 1
attach *OH: one red oxygen bonded to the surface and one white hydrogen bonded
to that oxygen. On slab 2 attach *O: one red oxygen bonded to the surface, no
hydrogen. On slab 3 attach *OOH: two red oxygen atoms joined by one O-O bond,
one white hydrogen on the terminal oxygen, and the other oxygen bonded to the
surface. Use simple circular atoms with crisp bonds, slight isometric view,
thin dark outlines, and one green ring under each attached intermediate. No
water layer, no bubbles, no electrode hardware, no reaction equation.

## Water Harvesting Panel

Create a clean icon-style scientific panel for atmospheric water harvesting by
first water adsorption. Show a manufactured porous adsorbent block on the
right with a regular geometric pore lattice, like engineered ceramic or
aluminosilicate material, not rock. Show exactly six isolated water molecules
moving from left to right toward the pores. Every H2O molecule is one red
oxygen plus two white hydrogens in a bent V shape with two O-H bonds and no
H-H bond. Exactly three water molecules are attached singly at green-highlighted
hydrophilic pore sites; the others remain in the vapor phase. No clusters, no
drops, no liquid film, no fog, no condensation. Use faint cyan motion streaks
only behind vapor molecules.

## Gas Separation Panel

Create a clean icon-style scientific panel for CO2/N2 gas separation by
selective adsorption. Show one engineered porous membrane or sorbent block with
regular pores. Show only two molecule types: CO2 and N2. Use exactly four CO2
molecules and four N2 molecules. CO2 is linear red-black-red with two C-O bonds
and 180 degree geometry. N2 is a blue-blue diatomic with one N-N bond. Two CO2
molecules are adsorbed inside pores at green highlighted sites. Two CO2
molecules are approaching the membrane. All four N2 molecules pass through with
faint cyan trails and no adsorption glow. No H2O, no methane, no extra species,
no dense cloud, no industrial equipment.

## Catalyst Poisoning Panel

Create a clean icon-style scientific panel for catalyst poisoning and
deactivation by strong adsorption. Use a split before/after composition with no
text: left half shows a clean matte metal catalyst surface with five green open
active-site rings and three simple reactant molecules approaching; right half
shows the same surface darkened after a yellow sulfur poison binds strongly to
the central site. On the poisoned side, the central site has an amber blocked
ring, neighboring active-site rings are dim green or gray, and reactant
molecules curve away or stop above the surface using simple dashed motion
paths. The message should be explicit: after poison adsorption, other active
species cannot attach. Use simplified atoms, bold outlines, flat lighting, no
photorealistic metal reflections, no skull icons, no labels.

## D3 Dispersion Panel

Create a clean icon-style panel representing DFT-D3 dispersion correction.
Show two separate neutral molecular fragments or small surface clusters facing
each other across a clear gap. Use simple matte graphite and white spheres
with crisp light-gray bonds. Between the fragments, show subtle curved
NVIDIA-green dotted attraction arcs indicating weak nonbonded dispersion. The
fragments should not form a chemical bond. No labels, equations, UI frame, or
logos.

## Electrostatics / PME Panel

Create a clean icon-style panel representing long-range electrostatics in a
periodic simulation cell. Show a dark periodic box or lattice slab with a few
simple charged particles: blue positive sites and red negative sites. Use
smooth cyan and NVIDIA-green field lines curving between opposite charges and
continuing through faint periodic ghost images at cell boundaries. No labels,
equations, UI frame, or logos.
